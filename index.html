About Me

Turning ideas into full‑stack software.

S

My path into software didn't start in computer science — I hold a B.E. in Electrical & Electronics Engineering. I moved into full‑stack development through a dedicated Java Full Stack training program at Tap Academy, which means I'm used to building my fundamentals deliberately rather than assuming them.

Today my core focus is backend engineering: Java, Spring Boot, Spring Security, JWT authentication, and JPA/Hibernate over MySQL, all wired together through clean REST APIs. I pair that with enough frontend fluency in HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and React to design and ship a project end to end on my own, without waiting on someone else to build the interface.

Somewhere along the way the frontend side stopped being “the other half” of full stack and became something I genuinely enjoy — I'll happily spend real time on interface polish, motion, and detail on projects where most people wouldn't notice if I hadn't. This site is one of them.

Outside of project work, I practice data structures & algorithms consistently, because I'd rather build that muscle on purpose than rely on whatever shows up in day-to-day feature work.
